Sandglass Life

Privacy policy

Sandglass Life is an iPhone app from Ootdcorp ltd. It records a private day on the phone. This policy describes version 1.0.

What stays on the iPhone

  • Precise location, including after you leave the app, so a day can include the places you went.
  • Motion, only to tell a walk from a drive.
  • Photos you already took. The app reads them on the device to attach a picture to a place. It does not upload them, change them, or save a copy off the phone.
  • The names you give places, and the days the app builds from the above.

What we do not do

There is no account, no server for your days, no advertising, and no tracking. Location, motion, and photos are used for the app itself. They are not linked to your identity and they are not sold.

The year

The last 7 days are included. Sandglass Life Annual is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your card number. Apple tells the app whether this Apple ID has an active year.

Erasing

Settings → Erase all days and places removes the days, places, and names stored by the app. Photos in your library stay. Uninstalling the app removes the same on-device record.
