The app

The app rebuilds days from location, motion, and photos already on the iPhone. A place name or a time can be wrong. You can rename a place and you can erase the record in Settings.

Sandglass Life Annual

  • Title: Sandglass Life Annual.
  • Length: 1 year. It renews each year until you cancel.
  • Price: the price shown in the app at purchase, currently $19.99 in the United States. Apple may show a different local price.
  • The last 7 days stay available without a subscription. A year unlocks every earlier day and the films of those days.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Renewal is charged within 24 hours before the year ends.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the year ends in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel the year.
  • Restore Purchases, in the app, puts an existing year back on this iPhone.

Privacy

Days stay on the iPhone. The privacy policy describes what the app reads.
