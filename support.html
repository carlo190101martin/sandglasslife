Sandglass Life

Support

Sandglass Life records a day on your iPhone. The last 7 days are included. Sandglass Life Annual keeps the earlier days.

To cancel a year, open Settings on the iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

To remove days stored by the app, open Sandglass Life → Settings → Erase all days and places. Photos in your library are not deleted.
